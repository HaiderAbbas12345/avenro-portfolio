This policy explains what personal information Avenro ("we", "us") collects when you use this website or contact us, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the website and respond to inquiries.

## Who we are

Avenro is an independent software and digital development studio. For any privacy question or request, contact us at [hello@avenro.com](mailto:hello@avenro.com).

## Information we collect

### Information you give us

When you use our contact form or email us, we receive the information you choose to share — typically your name, email address, company name, project type, project description and, optionally, a budget range and timeline.

### Website analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand how the website is used, such as which pages are visited, how visitors arrive and which calls to action are clicked. It is designed to be privacy-friendly: it does not use cookies and does not build profiles of individual visitors across websites. We use this information only in aggregate to improve the website.

### Server logs

Our hosting provider automatically processes technical information such as IP addresses, browser type and request times in order to deliver the website securely and to detect abuse. These logs are kept for a limited period by the provider.

## How we use information

- To respond to your inquiry and discuss potential projects.
- To operate, secure and improve this website.
- To meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations where applicable.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your inquiry details for unrelated marketing without your permission.

## Legal bases (UK and EU visitors)

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on our legitimate interests to operate the website and to respond to inquiries you send us, and on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract when you ask us about a project.

## Service providers

We use a small number of trusted providers to run the website and deliver messages:

- **Vercel** — website hosting and privacy-friendly analytics.
- **Resend** — delivery of contact-form submissions to our inbox.
- **Our email provider** — storing and managing email correspondence.

These providers process data on our behalf and only for the purposes described here. Some may process data outside your country; where that happens, we rely on the safeguards those providers offer under applicable law.

## How long we keep information

We keep inquiry correspondence for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the duration of the relationship and any period required for legal or accounting purposes. Inquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted when they are no longer needed.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to processing, and to receive a copy of your data. To make a request, email [hello@avenro.com](mailto:hello@avenro.com). You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our services change. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised.
