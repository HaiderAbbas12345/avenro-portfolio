## Overview

Lumen Metrics is a fictional B2B SaaS product we designed and built to show how we approach subscription software end to end: scoping a first release, designing data-heavy interfaces that stay readable, and engineering a multi-tenant platform. The product gives SaaS teams a clear view of recurring revenue, retention and their most valuable accounts.

## The Challenge

Early-stage SaaS companies usually have the data they need — in their billing provider, product database and CRM — but not in a form they can act on. Founders end up exporting CSVs into spreadsheets every month, and different people quote different numbers for the same metric.

The product challenge was to present a small set of trustworthy metrics clearly, without overwhelming users with every possible chart. The engineering challenge was to do it in a way that keeps each customer's data isolated and secure, and stays fast as data grows.

## Approach

We scoped the first release around three questions a SaaS team asks every week: how is revenue trending, are customers staying, and which accounts need attention? Every screen in the first release answers one of those questions; everything else went on a later roadmap.

For the interface, we designed a restrained visual system for data: one accent color for the primary measure, neutral tones for context, generous spacing and consistent number formatting. Charts were chosen for the question they answer, not for variety.

## Solution

The concept includes:

- **An overview dashboard** with headline revenue and retention metrics, a revenue trend and revenue by plan.
- **Cohort retention reports** with filters by plan and region, which can be saved and scheduled for delivery by email.
- **An accounts view** highlighting the largest and most at-risk customers.
- **Team workspaces** with roles and permissions, built on a multi-tenant data model.
- **Billing integration** with Stripe for plans, trials and subscription management.
- **A mobile overview** so founders can check key numbers from their phone.

## Technology

Next.js, React and TypeScript for the application, PostgreSQL with row-level tenant isolation, background jobs for data syncing and scheduled reports, Stripe for subscriptions, and Tailwind CSS with design tokens for a consistent component system.

## Result

As a concept, Lumen Metrics has no customers or usage data, and we do not present any. The outcome is a working, well-structured product prototype that demonstrates how we take a SaaS idea from a focused first release to an interface customers can understand at a glance — on a foundation designed to grow.
